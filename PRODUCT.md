# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

A small private group of friends who train and track food together. They open IronPlate mostly on a phone: between sets at the gym with a thumb and sometimes sweaty hands, or at a table logging a meal. Sessions are short and task-driven. A secondary audience is the owner, who also administers the app.

## Product Purpose

IronPlate combines a MyFitnessPal-style food diary with a Hevy-style workout logger and a small social feed, in one app for one friend group. Success is that logging a meal or a set takes seconds, the numbers can be trusted, and seeing friends' sessions makes everyone show up more.

## Positioning

The fastest honest way to log food and lifts, with friends in the same feed.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, encouraging, confident, and approachable. The voice is supportive and direct and celebrates consistency (never guilt, never streak-shaming). Copy is short and plain. A PR gets a mark, not confetti. The interface is built from cards with 12px corners (tighter than the brand package's 20px, by choice) with spacious sections and prominent stats.

## Anti-references

Cluttered all-in-one nutrition apps with ads, upsells, and dozens of competing widgets. Neon "gym bro" dashboards. Gamified wellness apps that scold. Generic SaaS-blue dashboards.

## Design Principles

1. Speed is the feature: the common action (log a set, log a food) is always one or two taps away and never behind a modal.
2. Numbers lead: weights, reps, calories, and macros are the largest, clearest things on screen.
3. Colour carries meaning: Midnight Navy for headings, navigation and primary buttons; Electric Lime for highlights, progress and accents; Soft Lavender for recovery content and secondary cards; Warm White as the background; Slate Grey for secondary text. Navy text on lime, warm white text on navy.
4. Built for a gym: big tap targets, readable at arm's length and under glare, forgiving of mistakes with easy undo.
5. Friends, not followers: social features are light and private-first, never a performance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follows the device's light or dark setting. The brand package defines the light theme; dark is derived from it. Body text meets 4.5:1 contrast in both themes. Tap targets are at least 44px. Motion respects reduced-motion. Calorie numbers can be hidden, and the app never shames missed days.
